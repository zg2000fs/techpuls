A Japanese pop-up is served by waitstaff with dementia

Japan’s Restaurant of Mistaken Orders tells diners their meals might be mixed up because the waitstaff are seniors with dementia. Organizers say the goal is understanding and connection, not perfect service.

A Japanese pop-up restaurant is served by waitstaff with dementia. Diners are told up front their orders might be mixed up, because the goal is understanding, not perfect service. In its latest event, four seniors aged 78 to 88 worked the room and chatted with guests. One woman cried seeing her mother active again, and one waitress left smiling, saying she was happy to talk with so many people.
