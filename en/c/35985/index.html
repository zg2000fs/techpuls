Chinese firefighting robot reportedly survived furnace

A Chinese company says its firefighting robot stayed operational for 30 minutes in a 1,000 degrees Celsius furnace. The test suggests promise for dangerous industrial fires, but real-world performance is still unknown.

A Chinese firefighting robot reportedly survived 30 minutes in a 1,000 degrees Celsius furnace. Its maker says it stayed operational the whole time, and it’s built for places too dangerous for human firefighters. The robot is remotely controlled and can shoot water or foam up to 85 meters. But a furnace test isn’t a real fire, so its performance with smoke and falling debris is still unknown.
