Claude may have helped prove Riemann hypothesis

A Harvard mathematician says Claude helped him find a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. He shared millions of lines of verification code, but the result still needs independent review.

A Harvard mathematician says Claude helped prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Levent Alpöge, who’s connected to Anthropic, says the AI-assisted proof tackles what many call math’s biggest unsolved problem, first posed more than 160 years ago. He hasn’t published a formal paper because he says the proof runs over 250,000 pages. Instead, he shared millions of lines of Lean code, but until other mathematicians verify it, the claim is still unconfirmed.
