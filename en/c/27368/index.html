Escape from Tarkov is heading to consoles

Escape from Tarkov is reportedly very close to getting a console version. Its developer says an official announcement should come early next year.

Escape from Tarkov is finally heading to consoles. One of the developers says the port is very close, and he’s fully confident it’ll be officially announced early next year. That would bring the extraction shooter beyond PC after nearly nine years in beta and its full 1.0 launch last November. If the console version runs better than the criticized PC build, some current PC players could reportedly switch too.
