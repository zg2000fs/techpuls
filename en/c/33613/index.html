Zheng Yuanjie returned with AI-made cartoons

Chinese children’s author Zheng Yuanjie has returned to social media after two years with AI-made animated shorts based on his own stories. He says AI can let writers replace animators and warned skeptics to try the best paid models.

China’s “fairy tale king” returned with AI-made animated shorts. After two years away, children’s author Zheng Yuanjie started posting free videos on Douyin, China’s version of TikTok, based on his own stories, even though he says he knows nothing about animation. Zheng argues AI lets writers do animators’ jobs, and says skeptics have probably only tried free tools. His warning is blunt: try the most expensive AI model, and you might lose sleep.
