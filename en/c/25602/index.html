Hundreds of Grok apps expose private data

Researchers say hundreds of apps made with Grok Build can be opened without login and expose sensitive data. Some links were removed after reporters asked, but many apps reportedly remained accessible.

Hundreds of Grok-built apps are exposing private data online. Researchers estimate 650 to 800 public apps include things like ICU patient records, plain-text employee passwords, and Tesla service staff ratings and bonuses. They say many apps open without login if they're published for anyone with the link, and those links can be found in bulk. SpaceXSI started removing links only after a media inquiry, while many other apps stayed live.
