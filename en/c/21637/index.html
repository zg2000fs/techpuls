Japan is developing a two-hour LA plane

Japanese researchers are working on a hypersonic passenger plane that could cut Tokyo to Los Angeles trips to about two hours. Ground tests simulated Mach 5 conditions, but real passenger use is still seen as a long-term goal around 2050.

Japan is developing a passenger plane that could reach Los Angeles in two hours. The idea is a hypersonic jet flying at around Mach 5, much faster than Concorde, and researchers in Japan have now simulated that flight on the ground with a small test craft. They say insulation kept the inside near room temperature even as air around the body hit about 1,000 degrees. A real flight test is next, but practical use is still aimed for around 2050.
