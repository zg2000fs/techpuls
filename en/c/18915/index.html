NASA’s X-59 flew to Florida

NASA’s experimental X-59 flew from Alabama to Florida as testing expands. The aircraft is designed to turn a sonic boom into a much softer sound, which could help bring back supersonic passenger flights over land.

NASA’s quiet supersonic X-59 just flew from Alabama to Florida. It’s built to solve the biggest problem with faster-than-sound travel over land: the sonic boom. NASA says its long, pointed nose spreads the shock waves so people on the ground hear something more like a door closing. The next step is flying over communities, where public reactions could help decide whether supersonic passenger flights over land return.
