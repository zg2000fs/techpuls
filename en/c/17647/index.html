A father used AI to turn his TV into a homework lock

A father in China reportedly built an AI-powered TV lock that pauses shows every 10 minutes until his son answers school questions correctly. After the son tried rebooting the TV to escape it, the father updated the system to keep the questions waiting.

A father used AI to turn his TV into a homework lock. Every 10 minutes, his son has to solve a math, Chinese, or English question before the show continues, and each wrong answer adds another task. The son tried unplugging the TV and rebooting it, but the father says he patched that too. Now the whole thing has reportedly become a running father-son battle to find the next loophole.
