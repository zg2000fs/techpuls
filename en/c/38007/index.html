Signal now blocks chat screenshots on iPhones

Signal's latest iPhone update blocks screenshots inside the app by default. The company says it's doing that to keep Siri AI from scanning chats, though users can turn the setting off.

Signal now blocks chat screenshots on iPhones by default. The new setting is meant to stop Siri AI from scanning your messages, and Signal says Apple doesn't give developers a better way to protect app content from Siri AI or other AI agents. If you want screenshots back, you can switch off Block Siri AI in Signal's privacy settings. The twist is Apple already has its own Siri AI app exclusion option, but Signal isn't relying on it.
