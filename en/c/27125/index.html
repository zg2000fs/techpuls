JD.com says its robot battery swaps in 10 seconds

JD.com says its upcoming robot battery can be swapped in 10 seconds without powering the robot down. The company also says two packs can run together to double runtime and reduce custom battery work for robot makers.

JD.com says its new robot battery swaps in 10 seconds without shutdown. The company plans to show it on November 15, and says robots can keep working while the old pack comes out and a new one goes in. Two packs can also run in parallel to reportedly double runtime. The bigger pitch is standardization: robot makers may be able to use one ready-made battery instead of redesigning one for every model.
