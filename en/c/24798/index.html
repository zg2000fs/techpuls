Apple’s next AirPods could get cameras

A longtime Apple supplier says it’s working on AirPods with cameras that would feed visual data to Apple’s AI. The same supplier is also preparing for Apple’s still-unannounced smart glasses.

Apple’s next AirPods could come with cameras. A longtime Apple supplier says it’s already working on them, and the cameras reportedly aren’t for taking photos. Instead, they’d send visual info from your surroundings to Apple’s AI system, so Siri could one day answer questions about a book, building, or sign you’re looking at. The same supplier is also preparing production for Apple’s unannounced smart glasses.
