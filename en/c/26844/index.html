Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen for free users

Google has unveiled Gemini 4 Hydrogen and says it is 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash while making fewer incorrect responses. The model is rolling out globally in the Gemini app and Google Search, including for free users.

Google just launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen for free users. Google says the new model is 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and gives incorrect answers up to five times less often. In the Gemini app and Google Search, it can now handle more complex requests, like planning a tiny studio layout with specific furniture and images instead of text-only suggestions. It’s also Google’s third new Gemini model in six weeks.
